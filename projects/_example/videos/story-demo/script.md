## Lines
1. Maya downloaded her fifth habit app on a rainy Sunday.
2. By Wednesday, she had forgotten it existed.
3. Then one morning, a text. Did you drink water today?
4. She replied yes. And the next day. And the next.
5. Loopa. Habits that check in.
