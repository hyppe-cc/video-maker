# Brand

**Loopa** is a habit tracker that texts you instead of making you open an app.

- One-liner: "Habits that check in with you."
- Personality: warm, a little cheeky, never preachy.
- Visual: indigo (#6366f1) + cyan (#22d3ee), big bold Inter, lots of air.
- Never: guilt-tripping, fitness-bro tone, before/after body shots.
