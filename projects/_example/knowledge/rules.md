# Content rules

- Hook in the first 1.5 s: a question or a "that's me" claim.
- 15–30 s. One idea per video.
- Captions: max 2 lines, highlight 1–2 words per line.
- Write numbers as words in script lines (TTS reads them better).
- No exclamation marks, no em dashes in captions.
- Keep all text above y≈1600 (platform UI covers the bottom).
- End on the product name + one URL.
